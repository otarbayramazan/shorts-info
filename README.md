# Shorts Uploader

Shorts Uploader is a small personal automation tool. It uploads the owner's own short videos
(YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels) to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

- Only the owner of the account uses it.
- It requests permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel only.
- It does not read, collect or share any personal data of other people.

See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for the privacy policy and [TERMS.md](TERMS.md) for the terms of use.
