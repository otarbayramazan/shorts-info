# Privacy Policy - Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos
to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

## What data is accessed
The app requests the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) for the owner's Google account.
It uses this permission only to upload videos chosen by the owner.

## What data is stored
Authorization tokens are stored in private encrypted secrets of the owner's own repository.
The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person.

## Third parties
Data is sent only to Google/YouTube and Meta/Instagram APIs in order to publish the owner's videos.
No analytics, advertising or other third-party services are used.

## Revoking access
The owner can revoke access at any time in the Google Account security settings
(https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in the Meta account settings.
